# TRUSTGUARD AI — KNOWN ARCHITECTURAL LIMITATIONS

> **Declaration:** *Radical honesty begins by disclosing our own boundaries. Below is a full, unfiltered disclosure of TrustGuard AI's current limitations.*

---

## 1. Passive Reconnaissance vs. Active Penetration Testing

TrustGuard AI performs **strictly non-invasive, passive reconnaissance**.
- **What We Do**: We inspect publicly presented TLS certificates, HTTP response headers, public DNS records, HTML DOM markup, and domain registration age.
- **What We Cannot Do**:
  - We do not execute active penetration testing, SQL injection fuzzing, or vulnerability exploitation against target servers.
  - We cannot inspect a target website's backend source code, server-side database configurations, or cloud storage ACLs.
  - A website marked `SAFE_LOOKING` may still harbor backend vulnerabilities (such as unpatched zero-days or database injection flaws) invisible to public HTTP requests.

---

## 2. Browser Sandbox Execution Limits

Because TrustGuard AI is delivered as a modern web application:
- It **cannot** detect malware residing on your personal computer's hard drive (`C:\` or `/`).
- It **cannot** see what other native applications (Zoom, Steam, Word) or browser processes are running on your machine.
- It **cannot** verify whether your operating system's native antivirus software (e.g. Windows Defender) is enabled or up-to-date.
- **Deception Warning**: Any web application claiming to perform these operations from a web browser without a native desktop companion agent is misleading you.

---

## 3. Threat Intelligence Database Latency

- Public reputation blocklists (such as Google Safe Browsing or URLhaus) operate on community discovery and automated crawling.
- **Zero-Day Phishing Window**: Newly registered phishing domains (created within the last 1-6 hours) may not yet appear on global blocklists.
- TrustGuard AI compensates for this latency using passive heuristics (Levenshtein lookalike brand detection, punycode parsing, new domain flags, missing security headers), but cannot guarantee detection of completely novel attack campaigns.

---

## 4. Probabilistic Nature of Large Language Models

- TrustGuard AI utilizes Google Gemini Flash to explain threats and format recommendations.
- While constrained by strict JSON schema definitions and output honesty filters, large language models are probabilistic reasoning systems.
- Guidance generated by the AI assistant should be treated as structured decision-support and verified against official service provider documentation.

---

## 5. Scope of Advice

- **Not Antivirus Software**: TrustGuard AI does not quarantine, remove, or execute real-time disinfection of malware on your operating system.
- **Not Legal Advice**: Emergency playbooks and privacy assessments are informational and do not constitute legal counsel or cyber-insurance claims representation.
- **Not Law Enforcement**: TrustGuard AI cannot track perpetrators or initiate criminal investigations. If you are experiencing extortion or physical threats, contact local law enforcement or national cyber emergency centers immediately (e.g., IC3.gov in the US or Action Fraud in the UK).
